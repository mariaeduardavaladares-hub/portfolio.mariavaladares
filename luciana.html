Tastes Like Memory

Brand strategy case · Brasília, Brazil · 2026

A cake that tastes like memory.

Bolo com gosto de lembrança.

Building a brand from zero for a home baker who makes the tall, cream-loaded party cakes Brazilians grew up with. The strategy turned nostalgia into her main advantage against chains, bakeries and Instagram cake designers.

Client
Luciana, home baker and grandmother
My role
Brand & creative strategy, naming, visual direction
Scope
Research, positioning, naming, launch campaign, content plan
Studio
Valadares Studio

Where it started

“Tia, pode fazer um bolo?”

“Auntie, could you make a cake?” A friend of her son was grieving and only wanted a cake. Luciana baked it. That request became the origin of the brand, and the clue to what her cakes really do for people.

The challenge

A crowded, growing market with no place for her yet.

Luciana had talent, a loyal circle and no brand: no name, no positioning, no plan to sell. The brief was to understand the market, find what makes her cake different, propose a name and identity, and build a content plan to start selling.

Instagram bakersMany, and looking more alike every month.
The white spaceA generous, homemade party cake made by a real person, at a fair price. Nobody owned it.

Cultural insight

For Brazilians, cake is never just dessert.

Celebration

The birthday, the win, the family party.

Comfort

The cake that arrives when words run out.

Gratitude

A way to say thank you without a speech.

Memory

The taste of grandma’s house and childhood.

Sharing

Made to be cut and divided.

Human insight

When we don’t know what to say, we bring a cake.

The cultural tension

The world got fast. People started missing things.

Vinyl, flip phones, rerun soap operas, family recipes. People are returning to things made slowly and full of history. Luciana already makes exactly what people are looking for: the 2000s party cake, tall and layered, with whipped cream and fruit, no fondant and no trends.

It is not nostalgia for things. It is nostalgia for people. A cake like the ones from back then is a way to bring someone back to the table.

The strategy

Chains sell cake. Luciana sells memory.

Positioning

The party cake made the old way, at home, to bring family memories back in happy moments and hard ones.

Strategic concept

A cake that tastes like memory.

Bolo com gosto de lembrança.

Brand role

The kindest way to say happy birthday, thank you, I’m sorry and I’m here.

Every cake goes out with a handwritten card.

Values
MemoryAbundanceCareFaith
Audience

Nostalgic families aged 30 to 55 who want the cake from their childhood; people looking after someone, in joy or grief; the church, neighbourhood and work circles where word of mouth happens; fair-goers who try a slice and come back to order.

Tone of voice

Warm, generous, simple, joyful, faithful with a light touch. Never gourmet, cold, preachy or full of English.

Naming

Six routes, each with its own slogan and palette.

Every route was checked against existing businesses in Brazil and flagged for trademark search before registration.

Route 1 · Personal signature

Luci Cake

“Feito pela Luci, do jeito que você lembra.”

Made by Luci, just the way you remember.

Route 2 · Faith and joy · Recommended

Bendito Bolo

“Abençoado em cada fatia.”

“Blessed Cake.” Blessed in every slice. Faith, humour and category in one Portuguese name.

Route 3 · Everyday faith

Bolo Nosso

“O bolo nosso de cada festa.”

From “our daily bread”: our cake for every party.

Route 4 · Family nickname

Bô Lu

“Bolo de vó, com carinho de Lu.”

Grandma’s cake, with Lu’s love. The way grandkids would say it.

Route 5 · Parisian inspiration

Loulou • Bolos

“Um mimo de antigamente.”

A little treat from the old days, with “Lu” inside the name.

Route 6 · Faith and gratitude

Graças ao Bolo

“Graças ao bolo, a família se junta.”

“Thanks to the cake”, a play on “thank God”. Thanks to the cake, the family gets together.

Launch campaign

Who would you bring a cake to?

Campaign POV: make Brasília think of Luciana whenever someone needs to say happy birthday, thank you, I’m sorry or I’m here.

Phase 1 · Teaser

The origin story

Luciana tells the “Auntie, could you make a cake?” story on video, before the name is revealed.

Phase 2 · Launch

Name and menu

The name and menu go live and orders open on WhatsApp.

Phase 3 · Sustain

A cake a week

People share who they would bring a cake to. Every week Luciana delivers one, with a handwritten card.

Content strategy

Real kitchen, real hands, real stories.

No stock photos or shop-window cakes. Window light in Luciana’s kitchen, her hands always in frame, the cut cake showing the filling, and the handwritten card as signature.

In Luciana’s kitchenBehind the scenes, process and her hands.
A cake for every momentMenu by occasion: birthday, comfort, gratitude.
Who received oneReal stories from people who got a cake, with permission.
Faith and affectionMessages of care and faith, without preaching.
Where to find herStreet fairs, cake jars and orders.

First month mapped week by week across Reels, carousels, daily Stories, WhatsApp Business and local fairs, followed by a launch checklist: trademark and handle checks, a menu of four to six signature cakes, full identity and health permits.
